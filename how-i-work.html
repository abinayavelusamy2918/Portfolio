03 — HOW I WORK

Run on RAID, not vibes.

Every program I lead comes back to the same four-letter framework — the one already on my desk daily.

R

Risks — surfaced early

I'd rather raise a risk that never materializes than discover one at go-live. Risk logs get reviewed, not filed.

A

Assumptions — made explicit

Unstated assumptions are where programs quietly drift. I write them down so they can be challenged, not inherited.

I

Issues — named, not softened

An issue that's named plainly gets solved faster than one that's managed around. Clear escalation paths, every time.

D

Dependencies — tracked end to end

Cross-team, cross-vendor, cross-market — dependencies are mapped before sprint one, not discovered mid-release.

The same lens, applied to product.

RAID isn't governance paperwork I keep on the side — each stage of the product cycle leans on a different letter of it.

01 — DISCOVERY

Frame the problem

User interviews, demand signals, and market context — before a line of scope gets written.

A — assumptions written down before they harden into scope
02 — ROADMAP

Sequence by value and risk

Prioritise on outcome, effort, and what each bet is blocked by — not on who asked loudest.

D — dependencies mapped before commitments are made
03 — DELIVERY

Ship in slices

Sprint execution and release planning, with scope held honest against the roadmap.

I — issues named plainly, with a live escalation path
04 — MEASURE

Close the loop

Adoption, CSAT, and retention read after every release — then fed back into the roadmap.

R — risks re-scored each cycle, not filed and forgotten
